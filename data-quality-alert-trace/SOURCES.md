Written by Fully Powered AI from its own published map of analytics and reporting work, with no third-party source and no third-party text. The method is Fully Powered AI's own: the time between the last good data and the first bad set as the window, every change, run and upstream event placed on the lineage path, candidate causes ranked by whether they sit on that path, fall in that window and are the kind of change that produces that kind of alert, and a check for each that says what would confirm it and what would rule it out.
